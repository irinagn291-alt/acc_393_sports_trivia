Launch on iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17.4 or later and tap Start a round on Play. No account or onboarding is required. Answer each question or let its 20-second timer run out, then advance through the 10 questions. Results show points, correct answers, and external reference links. To test saving, close a round, select Save and leave, and tap Resume round. The original deadline remains in effect.

Complete the Question of the day. The card stays disabled for the rest of the device's local day, and completion counts toward the streak even on a wrong answer or timeout. Earn 600 points in the first season chapter to unlock the next one.

In Duels, share a challenge code using the iOS share sheet. Enter it on a second installation to play the same ordered set independently. Share an eligible signed scorecard, paste it in the other installation's Duels or Ranking tab, and tap Add scorecard. The receiving app verifies the signature and recalculates the score locally. Ranking also shows local weekly points and recent rounds.

Reminders and automatic library updates are off initially. In More, tap Enable reminders to request permission for local notification alerts, sounds, and badges. Gameplay remains available if permission is denied. Notification taps open the daily question or Ranking. The app requires no camera, microphone, location, photo library, or biometric access.

In More, tap Update open library to request questions from Open Trivia DB over the network. With at least 10 questions cached and the interface set to English, tap Play English library. Optional background refresh makes the same request when scheduled by iOS. The 52 built-in questions work offline. WidgetKit reads saved progress from the shared App Group and opens the daily question or Ranking depending on the layout.
