On iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17.4 or later, launch the app and tap Start a round in Play. No account or onboarding is required. Answer or let the 20-second timer expire, then advance through all 10 questions. Results show points, correct answers and external reference links. To test resuming, close a round, choose Save and leave, and tap Resume round. The original deadline still applies.

Complete the Question of the day and check that the card stays disabled for the rest of the device-local day and the streak is recorded even after an incorrect answer or timeout. Score 600 points in the first season chapter to unlock the second.

In Duels, share a challenge code through the iOS share sheet and enter it on another installation to get the same ordered questions. Each player completes the round independently. Share an eligible signed scorecard, paste it into Duels or Ranking on the receiving installation and tap Add scorecard. The app verifies the signature and recalculates the score locally. Ranking shows local weekly points and recent rounds. Challenge and scorecard exchange use text sharing.

Reminders and automatic library updates start off. In More, Enable reminders requests notification permission for local alerts, sounds and badges. Denying permission leaves gameplay available. A reminder opens the daily question or Ranking. No camera, microphone, location, photo library or biometric access is required.

In More, Update open library sends a network request to Open Trivia DB. With at least 10 cached questions and the interface set to English, tap Play English library. The optional background refresh makes the same request when scheduled by iOS. The 52 built-in questions work offline. WidgetKit reads saved progress from the shared App Group and opens the daily question or Ranking depending on the layout.
