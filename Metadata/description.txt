Test your knowledge of league rules, sporting history and athletes with 52 built-in questions. Play a mixed round or choose a category to get 10 questions. You have 20 seconds per question and earn 100 points for each correct answer. Review the correct answers and follow the reference links after each round.

Play the question of the day to build your streak, or take on four season chapters. Score at least 600 points in a chapter to unlock the next one. You can replay chapters to improve your best score and save an unfinished round to resume later.

Share a challenge code so someone else can play the same 10 questions independently. Exchange signed scorecards to compare results, and see your weekly points and recent rounds in Ranking.

The built-in questions work offline. Optional reminders and widgets help you keep track of your progress. In the English interface, you can download and play additional English-language questions from Open Trivia DB.
