How well do you know league rules, sports history, and athletes? Play a mixed 10-question round or pick a category from 52 built-in questions. You get 20 seconds per question and 100 points for each correct answer. After the round, review the answers and open reference links to learn more.

Build a streak with the daily question, or play four season chapters. A score of 600 points or more unlocks the next chapter. Replay chapters to improve your best scores, and save an unfinished round to pick it up later.

Send a challenge code to a friend so you can each play the same 10 questions. Trade signed scorecards to compare results, and check your weekly points and recent rounds in Ranking.

Built-in questions are available offline. Optional reminders and widgets help you follow your progress. When the interface is set to English, you can download and play additional English-language questions from Open Trivia DB.
